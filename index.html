Unified Decent Work Architecture for Employee-Intensive Sectors

Whether on a high-altitude Construction site or inside a sterile Hospital ICU ward, frontline workers suffer from the same systemic vulnerabilities: unrecorded overtime, hidden wage deductions, unreported safety hazards, and lost paper credentials when moving between employers.

FairShift solves this through a single responsive PWA architecture that bridges frontline workers and site supervisors with real-time biometric and geo-fenced transparency, zero contractor cuts, and cross-enterprise portable skill passports.

🏗️ Construction Site (Metro Infra Corp)

  • Zero Contractor Skimming: Eliminates intermediary cuts on daily wages with transparent automated payslips (SDG 8.5).
  • Life-Safety Hazard Reporting: Instant voice transcription captures falling scaffolding or live wires in regional Hindi/Tamil.
  • Subcontractor Portability: Scaffolding & electrical licenses stay on the worker's passport when switching contractors.
  • Heat & Physical Fatigue Alerting: Flags shifts exceeding 48h to prevent fatal site accidents.

🏥 Hospital Support Staff (City General Hospital)

  • Night Shift Premium Guarantee: Auto-calculates +25% differential for sanitation & orderly night shifts.
  • Biohazard Exposure Whistleblowing: Anonymous logging protects workers reporting torn PPE and needle sharps incidents without retaliation.
  • Infection Control Credentials: Digital certification in WHO pathogen barriers prevents cross-ward contamination.
  • Burnout & Consecutive Shift Protection: Mandates 11h rest periods between intensive emergency shifts.
